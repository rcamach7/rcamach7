Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

Guildway is an iOS directory for discovering public Discord servers and bots. You can browse and report a listing without creating an account. Sign in with Apple is required only when you submit a server or bot for consideration.

Guildway does not track you.

Guildway does not use collected data to track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal information. Raw IP addresses and user-agent strings are not stored.

Data Guildway collects

Guildway declares and collects the following four data types:

Apple user ID — linked to you

When you use Sign in with Apple, Guildway stores Apple's stable subject identifier as the account key. It is used to create and secure your Guildway account and to associate your catalog submissions with that account. Your email address is never used as the account key.

Email address — linked to you and optional

Apple may provide an email address when you first sign in. Guildway stores it as optional account metadata for app functionality. It may be your relay address if you choose Hide My Email. You may also voluntarily put an email address or other contact information in the free-text contact field of a listing report; this is the only place a signed-out reporter is invited to provide contact information.

Other user content — linked to you

A catalog submission stores your proposed server or bot name, Discord destination, description, optional owner introduction, category, tags, and languages, along with its resolved Discord identity, review status, and moderation records. Because sign-in is required to submit, this content is linked to your Guildway account.

A listing report stores the listing, reason, optional details, optional contact field, and review status. Reporting does not require sign-in. A signed-out report stores no Guildway account ID; a report made while signed in may be associated with the account.

Product interaction — not linked to you, for analytics

Guildway records list impressions, listing-detail views, and outbound join or install actions to understand directory use and rank listings. Events include the listing, interaction type, time, and a rotating pseudonymous visitor value. They are not linked to your Guildway account.

Anonymous activity and abuse prevention

For analytics, Guildway derives a visitor pseudonym using HMAC-SHA-256 over the trusted network address and a bounded, normalized user-agent string. The pseudonym rotates every 1 day by default. Guildway stores the resulting pseudonym, not the raw IP address or user-agent string. It is used for event deduplication, rate limits, qualification, ranking integrity, and abuse analysis.

Reports also use a daily rotating HMAC-derived reporter value for rate limiting and duplicate detection. For a signed-out report it is derived from the network address; for a signed-in report it is derived from the Guildway user ID. Raw network addresses are not stored with reports.

Listings and public catalog information

A catalog listing may store and display its Discord ID, name, descriptions, optional owner introduction, Discord-hosted imagery, categories, tags, languages, controlled external links, and its validated Discord invite or bot-install destination. Server member and online counts and bot server/install counts are point-in-time observations with observation timestamps; they are not represented as live counts. Guildway also keeps moderation, destination-validation, publication, and revalidation records needed to operate the catalog.

Retention

  • Raw product-interaction events are retained for 90 days by default. Aggregate score windows and aggregate ranking anomaly/run records remain after the raw events expire.
  • The analytics visitor pseudonym rotates every 1 day by default.
  • Refresh-token families have a maximum lifetime of 30 days by default. Guildway stores hashes of refresh tokens, not the tokens themselves.
  • Expired or revoked session records are removed after a further 30 days by default under the stale-session retention setting.
  • Apple identity-token replay hashes are retained only through the identity token's expiration so that the same token cannot establish another session.
  • Listings, submissions, reports, moderation decisions, destination checks, and related safety records do not have a fixed short retention period. They are retained to operate, review, protect, and audit the public catalog, including after account deletion as described below.

Service providers and external services

Apple

Apple provides Sign in with Apple. Guildway verifies Apple identity tokens and does not hold an Apple access token or refresh token. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's own privacy terms.

Hosting

Railway hosts Guildway's API, worker, PostgreSQL database, and Redis infrastructure and processes data on Guildway's behalf to deliver and secure the service.

OpenAI moderation

Guildway uses OpenAI's moderation API as a third-party processor at launch. Submitted listing text and Discord-hosted icon or banner images may be sent to OpenAI for automated safety screening. Automated screening does not publish a submission; every submission also requires moderator review.

Discord links

If you choose to open a server invite or bot-install link, you leave Guildway and interact with Discord under Discord's own terms and privacy practices. Guildway is an independent directory and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord.

Delete your account

In the Guildway app, open Account > Delete Account and confirm deletion. Deletion clears the Apple subject and email from the account, marks the account deleted, and revokes every Guildway session belonging to it on every device.

The account row deliberately survives in scrubbed form. Catalog submissions and reports also survive, including their moderation and safety records, but they point to an account that identifies nobody. This preserves the record of why content was approved, rejected, or acted on. Signing in again with the same Apple ID creates a new Guildway account.
