Guildway

Support

Guildway is an iOS directory for finding public Discord servers and bots. Browsing and listing reports do not require an account. Sign in with Apple is required only to submit a server or bot for moderator consideration.

Need help?

Email contact@ricardo-camacho.dev. You should receive a response within 3 business days.

Report a listing

Open the listing in Guildway, select Report Listing, choose the reason, and add any useful details. You may report adult content, an unsafe destination, misleading information, spam, an invalid listing, or another concern. You do not need to sign in.

The contact field is optional. Add an email address or other contact information only if you want a moderator to be able to follow up. A report does not automatically hide a listing. A moderator reviews the report; unsafe-destination reports also trigger a fresh destination check.

Delete your account

In Guildway, open Account > Delete Account, then confirm the destructive action. Guildway clears the Apple subject and email from the account and revokes all of the account's sessions on every device.

Submissions, reports, and their moderation or safety records deliberately remain, but they point to a scrubbed account that identifies nobody. If you later sign in with the same Apple ID, Guildway creates a new account.

Before contacting support

  • Include a short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • For a listing issue, include the listing name or its Guildway link.
  • For an app error, include the iOS version and any request ID shown by the app.
  • Do not email passwords, Apple identity tokens, Guildway session tokens, or other secrets.
