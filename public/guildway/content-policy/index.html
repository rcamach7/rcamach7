Guildway submission terms

Content Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

These terms apply when you submit a Discord server or bot to Guildway. By submitting, you agree to this Content Policy. A submission is a request for review, not a promise that the server or bot will be added to the public catalog.

No submission is published automatically.

Every submission is screened and moderator-reviewed. Moderators may reject a submission or approve it only as a private draft. Publication is a separate decision and requires all safety, destination, completeness, and approval checks to pass.

What you may submit

You may submit a public Discord server or Discord bot when:

  • the server invite or bot authorization URL is a legitimate Discord HTTPS destination;
  • the destination resolves to the same server or bot represented by the submission;
  • the name, description, owner introduction, category, tags, and languages are accurate;
  • the content is suitable for Guildway's general or teen catalog and contains no adult content;
  • you have the right to provide the submitted text and destination for review; and
  • the submission does not impersonate, mislead, spam, or put users at risk.

Guildway resolves the destination to obtain the Discord identity, normalized invite or install URL, point-in-time count observations, and Discord-hosted imagery. You cannot set publication state, moderation state, scores, counts, or Discord identity from the client.

What is rejected or held for review

Guildway rejects content or destinations that include or promote:

  • adult-only, explicit sexual, erotic, hookup, or similar adult content;
  • sexual content involving minors or threatening hateful content;
  • malformed, non-HTTPS, mismatched, or otherwise unsafe Discord destinations;
  • malformed or unsafe external links;
  • identities, invite codes, destinations, domains, or submitter accounts subject to an active block; or
  • misleading representations, impersonation, spam, scams, or other unsafe or deceptive material.

Borderline provider-flagged content, custom redirects, risky bot permissions—including Administrator permission—and unexpected screening errors stay private for further review. Guildway may reject any submission that cannot be validated, does not fit the directory, or creates a safety or trust concern.

Screening and moderator review

Guildway applies deterministic text, URL, destination, identity, and active block-rule checks. At launch, submitted text and Discord-hosted icon or banner images may also be sent to OpenAI's moderation API for automated safety screening. Provider errors do not publish content. Automated approval is not final approval: every submission remains private until a moderator reviews it.

A moderator may reject a submission, merge an exact Discord identity with an existing listing without automatically replacing its public metadata, or approve a new private draft. Even an approved draft is not public until it separately satisfies Guildway's publication requirements and a moderator publishes it.

Accuracy and observed counts

Server member and online counts and bot server/install counts are point-in-time observations recorded with observation timestamps. They are not live counts. You must not attempt to manipulate counts, rankings, events, moderation results, or any other catalog signal.

Reporting a published listing

Open the listing in Guildway and select Report Listing. Choose a reason and provide useful details. Reporting does not require an account, and contact information is optional. Reports may cover adult content, unsafe destinations, misleading information, spam, invalid listings, or another concern.

A report does not automatically hide a listing. Moderators review reports and may resolve or dismiss them without changing visibility, or may explicitly hide or reject a listing. Unsafe-destination reports trigger a fresh destination check; repeated confirmed destination failures can independently hide or invalidate a listing.

Removal and enforcement

Guildway may reject a pending submission or hide, invalidate, reject, or permanently remove a published listing when it violates this policy, fails destination checks, becomes unsafe, or no longer meets publication requirements. Moderation actions require a reason and are recorded for safety and audit purposes. Removal from Guildway affects only the Guildway directory; Guildway does not control the underlying Discord server or bot.

Questions about a decision may be sent to contact@ricardo-camacho.dev. Include the listing or submission name and enough context to identify it. A response should arrive within 3 business days.

Independent service

Guildway is an independent directory and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord. Discord servers and bots are operated by their respective owners, not Guildway.
